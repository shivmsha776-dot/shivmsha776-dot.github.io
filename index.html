Channel Hub

Uses YouTube API Services

Channel Hub is a private desktop tool used by the owner of a few YouTube channels to upload their own finished videos to their own channels on a schedule.

What it does

  • Watches a folder on the owner's computer for finished videos.
  • Works out which of the owner's channels each video belongs to from the channel watermark in the video.
  • Uploads it to that YouTube channel with the YouTube Data API, scheduled at the owner's posting times, with the owner's thumbnail and playlist.

It runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. Access to each channel is given through Google sign-in and can be removed at any time.

By using Channel Hub you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
